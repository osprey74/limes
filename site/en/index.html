The speed limit of the road you are on, with its confidence.

Limes estimates the speed limit of the road you are driving on from open data and shows it next to your GPS speed. Where a limit is posted in the regulation data it shows that limit; otherwise it shows the statutory one. For roads in Japan.

Confidence

Every number comes with its confidence

The limit is estimated from open traffic-regulation data and map data. Each number is shown with a confidence of high, medium or low and the grounds it rests on. When the limit cannot be determined, no number is shown.

  • 50
    High

    Posted limit

    Roads with a limit in the traffic-regulation data. That limit is shown.

  • 60
    Medium

    Inferred from lane markings

    Roads with no posted limit that appear to have a centre line or lanes. The statutory 60 is shown.

  • 30
    Low

    No evidence of a centre line

    Ordinary roads with no such evidence get the cautious 30, with the ring drawn fainter.

  • —
    —

    Unknown

    When the road cannot be identified, or there is no data for it, no number is shown.

By design

Made to stay out of the way

It is an app you glance at while driving, so all it does is display.

  • No warnings, no sounds

    There is no speeding alert, no change of color and no sound. The limit and your speed are shown side by side, and that is all.

  • Nothing to operate while driving

    There is no map, and the driving screen only displays. It works in portrait and in landscape.

  • No account

    No name or e-mail address is asked for. The app contains no advertising or analytics.

  • Your location is used on the device

    Finding the road and its limit happens on the device, and your location is not sent to the developer's server. See the privacy policy for details.

Screens

These are all the screens

Besides the driving screen there are four: a word on safe driving at launch, the settings, the data downloads and the plans.

Data

Only the prefectures you need

The road data is split by prefecture. You download the ones you drive in.

  • All 47 prefectures

    Download and delete by prefecture or by region. The app can also add the prefecture you are in automatically.

  • Updated automatically

    The app checks for a new version at launch and once a day, and fetches it. By default it does so on Wi-Fi only.

Please note

Before you use it

The speed limit shown is an estimate from open data. It can differ from the real one: temporary limits for road works or weather, variable signs, and changes not yet in the data.

Always follow the road signs and markings in front of you. Do not operate the screen or keep your eyes on it while driving.

For use in Japan only. There is no data for roads elsewhere, and the app itself is in Japanese.
